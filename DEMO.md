# 90-Second Hackathon Demo

## 0:00–0:10 — Hook

Say: “Mind Meld Mayhem is a game about predicting your friends, not knowing facts. Everyone answers privately, and the room rewards you when your brains line up.”

Open the player page at `http://localhost:3000/` and create a room.

## 0:10–0:20 — Make the room visible

Share the six-character room code. Open the projector spectator view on the big screen:

`http://localhost:3000/?projector=1&room=ROOMCODE`

Say: “The big screen is the game show. Phones are the private controllers.”

## 0:20–0:35 — Trigger the Perfect Meld

Use two or more player tabs/devices. Start the game. On the first prompt, have every player type the exact same answer, ideally `pizza` or `apple`, then submit.

Say: “Nobody can see the other answers. The only strategy is reading the room.”

## 0:35–0:50 — Sell the reveal

As the answers reveal, say: “This is the moment: not one person got the answer right—everyone got inside everyone else’s head.”

Point out the grouped answer, player count, animated `+` score, glow, and confetti. Call out: “That is a Perfect Meld: the whole room thought alike.”

## 0:50–1:05 — Show competition

On the next round, intentionally submit the same answer from at least two players again to show a streak. On Risky Mind, choose `RISK IT` for one player and have two players use the same answer.

Say: “Matching is the core skill, but the streak and risk choices create a real comeback story.”

## 1:05–1:20 — Show the engineering

Trigger a reaction from a phone and point to the projector leaderboard. Briefly say: “The server owns the scores, timers, matching, reconnection identity, and rate limits. The browser is only a controller.”

## 1:20–1:30 — Close on the pitch

Say: “Mind Meld Mayhem turns the social skill of reading a room into a fast game-show moment: join, think, meld, score, and try not to lose the final round.”
